# Name exploration

A replacement for "Murmur". I wrote 142 candidates, grouped by where they come from, and then picked a top 10.

## Top 10 (ranked)

Domains were checked with GoDaddy on Sep 28, 2026. "Available" means it showed as open to register. Price and trademark were **not** checked.

| # | Name | Why it works | Domains found open |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wren** ⭐ | The wren is one of the smallest songbirds and one of the loudest. That's the product: a small, calm tool that makes you heard. It keeps the bird link that X/Twitter writers know, it's 4 letters, and it looks beautiful in EB Garamond. | wren.ai, getwren.com |
| 2 | **Plume** | A feather pen, and a plume of smoke rising (reach). Elegant, editorial, very Wispr-like. | plume.app, tryplume.com, useplume.com |
| 3 | **Stanza** | A thread is a poem in stanzas. Rhythmic, warm, and it sounds like a product you'd pay for. | stanza.app, getstanza.com |
| 4 | **Lede** | Journalism's word for the opening line, which is literally the hook. Smartest fit of all, but every domain I tried was taken. | none found |
| 5 | **Hone** | A verb: sharpen your writing. Short and confident. | hone.app |
| 6 | **Verso** | The back page, and "verse". Literary with a modern edge. | tryverso.com |
| 7 | **Byline** | Your name on the post. Strong for creators building a personal brand. | getbyline.com |
| 8 | **Nib** | The tip of a pen. Tiny, cute, a great icon. Domains taken. | none found |
| 9 | **Wordloom** | Weave words into threads. The only one with a clean `.com` open. | wordloom.com |
| 10 | **Tern** | A seabird that travels farther than any other. Reach, in 4 letters. | gettern.com |

**My pick: Wren.** It has the best story ("small bird, big voice"), it continues the current promise ("the quiet place to write loud posts"), and `wren.ai` is open.
