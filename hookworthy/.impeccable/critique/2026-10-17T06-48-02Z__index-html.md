- **P0 Identity dead end.** A user who skipped the handle could never set one; previews showed a bare "@".
- **P1 Server dead ends.** Connect X, Turn on, Email Sundays, Breakout alerts and Self-hosting all ended in setup instructions in a demo with no server.
- **P1 The signature was missing from Write.** No wavy soft-word line, no Sharpen; instead a hidden grade ("Nearly there · 2 of 5").
- **P1 New users met a wall of "nothing" and a stranger's numbers** on Week, Results and Voice.
- **P1 Draft my week's Schedule didn't schedule**, and its starters read badly ("rules I follow for revenge trading").
