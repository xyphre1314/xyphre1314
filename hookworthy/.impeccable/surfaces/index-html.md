# Surface brief: Hookworthy site + app (index.html)

Scope: the homepage and sign-in (Persuade) and the app routes (Operate), in one file.
Audience: creators who write in public (crypto and trading writers first), founders who post, ghostwriters.
Action: see their own kind of post get sharper in the first viewport, believe it stays their voice, start free.
Proof available: the product itself. Rasters: portraits (assets/people/avatars.jpg, avatars-2.jpg) and photos (assets/people/photos.jpg: desk, whiteboard, run, cafe, team, notes) were generated in Higgsfield (gpt_image_2_5, project "Hookworthy homepage assets") in round 12 and ship as sprite sheets; you.jpg is the owner's own avatar. No real users, numbers or quotes; people on the page are fictional with generated portraits (assets/people, provenance recorded in earlier rounds).
Constraints: single file, no build step, WCAG AA in both themes, reduced motion, nothing live for X (demo only).

Round 57 brief (owner): round 56 read worse than round 55. Combine round 55 (dark system, Newsreader + Inter, product in a window, bento) with the original pre-55 homepage (photo tiles inside the headline, the "11 PM thought" chapter, persona tabs, pricing cards, paste tool, giant wordmark). Senior-team craft; details matter. Owner chose the first screen: "Photo headline + live app". Image generation was offline when the choice landed, so the build is code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One sentence with faces in it, then the app doing the work. "Sound like you. Show up every day." The page refuses eyebrows, logo walls, testimonials, icon-card grids and any reach promise.

OWN-WORLD: Round 55's near-black world (page #0D0D0D, cards #171717, hairlines, ink #EDEDED, one light pill button), Newsreader display, Inter for UI, Geist Mono only for times and counts. From the original: real portrait and platform tiles set inside headlines, two-tone heads (ink, then grey), one inverted paper chapter, the amber wavy underline as the soft-word signal, green only for "better".

STORY: hero with live app; paper chapter "From an 11 PM thought to Tuesday morning" (five steps over a night-desk photo that turns to a sunrise run); slices of the product; "For anyone with a take" tabs; nothing goes out without you; three pricing cards; questions; paste your line; "End of the page. Start of the post." over a giant wordmark.

FIRST VIEWPORT: floating capsule nav; centred Newsreader H1 up to 96px on two lines, a portrait tile before "you" (faces turn over) and a platform tile after "Show up" (X, Threads, LinkedIn, Bluesky); one-line sub; light "Start free" pill and "See how it works"; the app window's tabs and top edge rise into the bottom of the screen. Signature interaction: in the window, soft words get the wavy line one by one, a cursor presses Sharpen, the first line resets, the X preview follows, a cursor presses Schedule and "Scheduled for Tue, 8:40 AM" lands.

FORM: owner-pinned combination (round 55 × the original), no seed roll because the brief pins both the world and the composition. The first screen was the owner's own pick in the round-57 question, verbatim: "Photo headline + live app (Recommended)". Code-led, because image generation was offline when the choice landed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
