# Afterglow

**Idea:** X's black room. One warm beam lands on a tilted glass app window, where fluff gets struck and the sharp post pulls into focus.

**Font:** Inter Display + Inter Text from one opsz file already in /fonts. Display is tight and crisp at 80px, and Text stays legible at 13px. It's Linear's own marketing face: neutral, no quirks.

**Palette:** #08090A ground · #F7F8F8 soft white · #A4A8AF line two, sub · #868B93 meta · #FFAA80 ember, used only as light and the fluff underline.

**Premium move:** the beam touches the product. The window's 1px gradient rim lights into a warm specular line where it lands.

**Borrows:** Linear (type, tilted product, easing), Superhuman (warm light on black), X (post card, white pill).
