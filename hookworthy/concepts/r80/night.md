# Night Keynote (night)

**Idea:** X's black, lit like an Apple keynote. Your draft and its sharper post float under one soft spotlight.

**Font:** Inter Display (InterDisplay-Variable.woff2, opsz 14–32), 740, tracked −0.047em. The same file at text size runs the UI. Crisp and neutral, close to X's grotesk without copying it.

**Palette:** #000 ground · #F5F5F7 punchline, pills · #86868B set-up line, micro · #A1A1A6 sub · X dark #E7E9EA, #7D8287, #2F3336 in cards · #FF9F0A fluff squiggle, the only colour.

**Premium move:** Light. An overhead cone, edge-lit borders with a specular top line, layered shadows, grain only where light falls.

**Borrowed:** Apple's grey-to-white headline and product under light. X's compose box, post card and white Post pill. Vercel's restraint.
