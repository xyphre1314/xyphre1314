# Tab to sharpen

**Idea:** Cursor's inline suggestion for writers. In an X-style compose box the fluff gets a squiggle, the cuts are struck with a `Tab ⇥ to sharpen` hint, it accepts itself, and the sharp post lands as the X card.

**Font:** Geist throughout (crisp, neutral, precise). Geist Mono only for the key hint and the time.

**Palette:** #000 page · #0A0A0A cards · #EDEDED text · #A1A1A1/#8F8F8F secondary · #1A1A1A hairlines · #5C5C5C crosshairs · #FFB224 fluff squiggle (only colour).

**Premium move:** A hairline grid with crosshairs and exact alignment: compose top lines up with the H1's cap height, post bottom with the micro line's baseline.

**Borrows:** Vercel's grid and two-tone H1, Cursor's Tab, X's black and white pill.
