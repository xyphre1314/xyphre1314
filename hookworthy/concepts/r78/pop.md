# Tangerine Pen (pop)

**Idea:** Hookworthy is the friend with the tangerine pen: it squiggles the fluff and slaps a sticker on the hook.

**Fonts:** Bricolage Grotesque 700/800 for display and wordmark: ink traps and a hand-made wobble give it a voice without a serif; its width axis (84% on phones) keeps every heading to two lines. Onest 400–650 for text: open, rounded, friendly at 15px.

**Palette:** Paper #FFFAF4 ground · Oat #F5EEE4 trays · Ink #1A1714 text, edges, key shadows · Ink 2 #5C544B secondary · Tangerine #FF6A13 CTAs, stickers (ink text only) · Pen #EB5600 fluff squiggle, strikes · Lilac #D3C8FF fields, picked chips, “why it hits” notes · Grape #4B33C9 second heading line, icons. Posts stay light-mode X.

**Typefully-like:** light ground, centred two-line headline, pill CTA, compose → post as the hero.

**Better:** an ownable mark (a squiggle that turns into a hook), the sticker on “hookworthy”, ink-edged objects with a pressable key shadow, personas as mini X profiles.

**Borrowed:** Wispr Flow’s warm off-white and human voice; Mobbin trends: sticker badges (Gumroad, Zendesk), dotted canvas, two-tone headlines, one bold colour field per moment.
