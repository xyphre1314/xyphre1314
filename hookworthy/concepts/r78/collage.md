# Paper Sky (collage)

**Idea:** Your draft is the paper. Hookworthy cuts the fluff, and what's left goes to the feed. Typefully's sky blue, made by hand.

**Fonts:** Gloock for display: a high-contrast editorial serif, like a headline cut from a magazine; one weight keeps it disciplined. DM Sans for text: friendly, geometric, quiet enough for X-style posts.

**Palette:** Ink #0F1419 (text, primary pill), Slate #536471 (secondary), Dusk #3F5F80 (second headline line), Sky #B9DBFB (grained sky), Kite #5AA6F2 (mark, scraps), Sky ink #1663BE (links, focus), Paper #FFFEFA, Butter #FFE8A3 (Why it hits), Blush #FFDAE2, Mint #D3F0DF, Lilac #E5E0FF, Marigold #EE9E0A (fluff squiggle), Pencil #2F86F0 (the cut).

**Typefully-like:** light page, sky wash, centred two-line headline, pill buttons, blue mark, product first.

**Better:** the hero tells the whole story (draft, cut, post, note). Paper pieces carry content (notes, tape labels, an index card), not decoration. The mark is a sticky note with a hook.

**Borrowed:** Craft's grainy sky and torn paper. Wispr Flow's warm serif and human tone. Mobbin trends: black pills, pastel boards, a polaroid, FigJam-style notes.
