# First Light (sky)

**Idea:** Wavy means fluff, straight means sharp, and everything else is clear air.

**Fonts:** Instrument Sans (variable, 95% width, 590) for display and UI, plus one Instrument Serif Italic word per headline. They come from the same designer, share x-height and cap height, and the italic sits on the sans' line metrics, so nothing shifts. The pair is crisper and more ownable than Inter. Tabular figures handle times, so there's no mono.

**Palette:** Cobalt #0B63E8 (action, 5.3:1 with white) · Ink #0C1424 · Slate #465062 · Haze #5F6879 · Sky wash #D5E6FF → white · Sky 100 #E6F0FF · Mist #F6F9FE · Hairline #E9EEF5 · Marigold #CC7A00 (squiggle) / #8F5600 (text) · Mint #0A7F55 · X feed #0F1419 / #536471 / #EFF3F4.

**Like Typefully:** light, a sky wash at the top, a centred two-line headline, one blue pill, bordered cards.

**Better:** a hook mark, a glass pill nav, a glass hero tray around real X light-mode cards, layered cool shadows, fine grain, and the wavy/straight line system.

**Borrowed:** Wispr Flow's serif-italic warmth; from Mobbin, glass pill navs (Craft, mymind) and grainy sky gradients (Craft, Air).
