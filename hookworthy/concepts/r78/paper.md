# Margin Notes (paper)

**Idea:** the editor in your margin. It squiggles the fluff, crosses it out in plum ink and leaves your words standing.

**Fonts**
- **Fraunces** (variable, Soft 100, optical size auto): a warm, rounded editorial serif. Its italic is the editor's voice: second heading lines and margin notes like "Why it hits".
- **Figtree** (variable): a friendly, clean sans for posts, UI and body text. Tabular figures, no mono.

**Palette**
- Paper `#FAF6EE`: page. Ink `#1C1A17`: text and primary pill.
- Plum ink `#4A2A7A`: marks, Sharpen, Approve and the final block (11:1 with white).
- Amber `#C98110`: the fluff squiggle only.
- Soft blocks: lilac `#ECE4F6`, butter `#FBEFC9`, sage `#E2ECDB`, blush `#F8E2D5`.
- Posts: X light.

**Like Typefully:** light and friendly, pills, and the product up front.

**Better:** an ownable hook mark, hand marks that mean something in the product (underline, strike, circle, arrow), a paper-stack hero and editorial type.

**Borrowed:** the cream ground and character serif of Wispr Flow and Granola, Craft's pastel note cards, and Mobbin's small black pill.
