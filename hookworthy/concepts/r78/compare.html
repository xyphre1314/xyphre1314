Four new looks for Hookworthy

Each direction keeps the same homepage, words and live Sharpen, and changes the fonts, colours, logo and style. All four sit in Typefully’s light, friendly family; open each one to scroll the full page.

Paper Sky

My pick

Typefully’s sky blue, made by hand. Your draft is the paper; the fluff gets cut; what’s left goes to the feed.

Fonts

Gloock (display) + DM Sans (text)

Colours
Ink #0F1419Sky #B9DBFBKite #5AA6F2Sky ink #1663BEPaper #FFFEFAButter #FFE8A3Blush #FFDAE2Marigold #EE9E0A
Like Typefully

Light page, sky wash, centred two-line headline, pill buttons, blue mark, product first.

Better than Typefully

Torn paper, sticky notes and a tape label tell the whole sharpen story in the first screen; a polaroid desk, index-card client voice, torn “Auto-…” strips.

Weakest point

Most hand-built of the four: plainer between about 1080–1180 px wide, and the phone hero is less tactile than desktop.

Like Typefully

The closest to Typefully: white page, sky wash, centred headline, one blue pill, bordered cards.

Better than Typefully

Glass nav and a glass tray holding draft → post, a tilted hook mark, layered cool shadows and grain; the easiest to carry into the app.

Weakest point

The safest lane: its character is in small details, so next to the others it can read as “very polished Typefully”.

Typefully’s friendliness, pills and product-first hero.

Better than Typefully

Wispr Flow-style warmth: editorial serif with real character, hand-drawn pen marks that are the product itself, pastel index cards, one plum finale.

Like Typefully

Light ground, centred two-line headline, pill CTA, product as hero.

Better than Typefully

The most personality: “hookworthy” on a tilted sticker, ink-edged cards with press-in shadows, personas as mini X profiles.

Weakest point

The loudest: tangerine plus grape headings is a lot next to warm paper, and the scalloped mark gets muddy at favicon size.

Every direction passes the same checks: no layout errors at phone or desktop width, headings two lines max, WCAG AA (axe: 0 issues), finished layout with reduced motion, and the live Sharpen still rewrites real lines.
