PrivacyTerms

Privacy.
Your drafts stay with you.

What stays in your browser

Drafts, saved ideas, your voice settings and the posts you bring are kept in this browser. Imports from Typefully, your X archive or a CSV are read right here, not uploaded to read them.

What leaves, and when

Only what you ask for. A rewrite with Claude sends that text to Claude; Quick mode stays on your device. A post you schedule goes to X or LinkedIn at the time you picked, once you connect them. Sync, if you turn it on, is encrypted on this device before it goes through your Hookworthy server.

What never happens

Nothing posts unless you schedule it. Hookworthy never sends DMs. There are no ads and no trackers on these pages.

Clearing it

Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes everything kept here. Anything you synced lives on your Hookworthy server until you remove it there.

Terms.
Your words, your call.

Your words are yours

What you write and bring stays yours. Hookworthy uses it to score, rewrite and schedule your posts.

You decide what goes out

Every post goes out because you scheduled it or posted it. Each platform’s own rules still apply to what you post there.

Not financial advice

Hookworthy helps you write about markets. It doesn’t give financial advice, and a better first line isn’t a trade signal.

Scores are a guide

The hook score is five checks on your words. It can’t promise reach, and it doesn’t try to.

Plans and usage

Usage counts only when Claude answers, so an error or a cancel costs nothing. Free stays free.
