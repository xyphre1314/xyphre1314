Privacy.
Your drafts stay with you.

What stays in your browser

Drafts, saved ideas, your voice settings and the posts you bring are kept in this browser. Turn on sync and they’re encrypted on this device before they reach Hookworthy’s servers, so they come back when you sign in. Imports from Typefully, your X archive or a CSV are read right here, not uploaded to read them.

What leaves, and when

Sharpen sends your first line to Claude through Hookworthy’s servers. Past a daily fair-use limit, or when Claude can’t answer, a lighter check runs on your device. A rewrite, or replies you ask to have drafted, send that text to Claude. On Pro and Studio, a post that takes off sends its first replies to Claude, so the alert arrives with them drafted. Quick mode stays on your device. A post you schedule goes to X or LinkedIn at the time you picked, once you connect them.

What never happens

Nothing posts unless you schedule it. Hookworthy never sends DMs. There are no ads and no trackers on these pages.

Clearing it

Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes everything kept here. Anything you synced stays in your account, encrypted, until you delete it there.

Terms.
Your words, your call.

Your words are yours

What you write and bring stays yours. Hookworthy uses it to sharpen, rewrite and schedule your posts.

You decide what goes out

Every post goes out because you scheduled it or posted it. Each platform’s own rules still apply to what you post there.

Not financial advice

Hookworthy helps you write about markets. It doesn’t give financial advice, and a better first line isn’t a trade signal.

Sharpen is a guide

It cuts and checks your words. It can’t promise reach, and it doesn’t try to.

Plans and usage

Usage counts only when Claude answers, so an error or a cancel costs nothing. Replies drafted when you ask use 1 rewrite for up to ten. On Pro and Studio, the first replies drafted on a post that takes off don’t use rewrites. Free stays free.
