Before you hand it
your first line.

  • It learns from your own posts: paste 8, or import your X archive. It keeps your lowercase and your numbers, and it never uses the words on your never-say list. Every cut is marked, so you can put any word back.

  • Keep it if you like: Sharpen is free, whatever you post with. Or bring your posts along: drop in a Typefully or CSV export, your X archive, or paste them in. They’re read in your browser, and your past posts teach Hookworthy how you open and when your people reply.

  • Only what you schedule, at the time you pick. X and LinkedIn posts go out then; Threads and Bluesky get a nudge with the post ready to paste.

  • It’s AI that edits your line, not AI that writes it. Sharpen cuts fluff, puts the point first and trades a wordy phrase for a plain one. Rewrites build three takes from your words, and a check rejects any version that changes your facts.

    In the app, Claude makes the edit, but this demo runs on your device, without Claude. Nothing goes out until you send it.
