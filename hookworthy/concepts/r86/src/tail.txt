<section class="sec" id="faq" aria-labelledby="faq-h">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="head">
      <h2 class="h2" id="faq-h">Before you hand it<br><span class="d">your first line.</span></h2>
    </div>
    <ul class="qa">
      <li><h3><button type="button" id="qb1" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="qa1">Will it sound like me?<span class="pm" aria-hidden="true"><svg class="ico b"><use href="#i-plus"/></svg></span></button></h3>
        <div class="a" id="qa1" role="region" aria-labelledby="qb1" hidden><p>It learns from your own posts: paste 8, or import your X archive. It keeps your lowercase and your numbers, and it never uses the words on your never-say list. Every cut is marked, so you can put any word back.</p></div></li>
      <li id="switch"><h3><button type="button" id="qb6" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="qa6">I already use a scheduler.<span class="pm" aria-hidden="true"><svg class="ico b"><use href="#i-plus"/></svg></span></button></h3>
        <div class="a" id="qa6" role="region" aria-labelledby="qb6" hidden><p>Keep it if you like: Sharpen is free, whatever you post with. Or bring your posts along: drop in a Typefully or CSV export, your X archive, or paste them in. They’re read in your browser, and your past posts teach Hookworthy how you open and when your people reply.</p></div></li>
      <li><h3><button type="button" id="qb2" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="qa2">Does it post without me?<span class="pm" aria-hidden="true"><svg class="ico b"><use href="#i-plus"/></svg></span></button></h3>
        <div class="a" id="qa2" role="region" aria-labelledby="qb2" hidden><p>Only what you schedule, at the time you pick. X and LinkedIn posts go out then; Threads and Bluesky get a nudge with the post ready to paste.</p></div></li>
      <li><h3><button type="button" id="qb3" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="qa3">Is it just AI writing, like ChatGPT?<span class="pm" aria-hidden="true"><svg class="ico b"><use href="#i-plus"/></svg></span></button></h3>
        <div class="a" id="qa3" role="region" aria-labelledby="qb3" hidden><p>It’s AI that edits your line, not AI that writes it. Sharpen cuts fluff, puts the point first and trades a wordy phrase for a plain one. Rewrites build three takes from your words, and a check rejects any version that changes your facts.</p><p>In the app, Claude makes the edit, but this demo runs on your device, without Claude. Nothing goes out until you send it.</p></div></li>
      <li><h3><button type="button" id="qb5" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="qa5">Is my writing used to train AI?<span class="pm" aria-hidden="true"><svg class="ico b"><use href="#i-plus"/></svg></span></button></h3>
        <div class="a" id="qa5" role="region" aria-labelledby="qb5" hidden><p>No. Hookworthy doesn’t train any model on your posts. What you send to Claude goes through Anthropic’s API, and Anthropic doesn’t train on API inputs by default.</p></div></li>
      <li><h3><button type="button" id="qb4" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="qa4">What about my clients’ accounts?<span class="pm" aria-hidden="true"><svg class="ico b"><use href="#i-plus"/></svg></span></button></h3>
        <div class="a" id="qa4" role="region" aria-labelledby="qb4" hidden><p>On Studio, each client gets their own voice and a review link. Nobody shares a password: clients sign in with X or Google to comment and approve. Once a client connects the X account they already have, their posts go out from it, never yours. Until then, a post waits for you to post it.</p></div></li>
    </ul>
