        <div class="a" id="qa1" role="region" aria-labelledby="qb1" hidden><p>It learns from your own posts: paste 8, or import your X archive.         <div class="a" id="qa6" role="region" aria-labelledby="qb6" hidden><p>Yes. Drop in your X archive, a Typefully or CSV export, or paste posts in. They’re read in your browser, and they teach Hookworthy how you open and when your people reply.</p></div></li>
      <li><h3><button type="button" id="qb2" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="qa2">Does it post without me?<span class="pm" aria-hidden="true"><svg class="ico b"><use href="#i-plus"/></svg></span></button></h3>

X and LinkedIn posts go out then; Threads and Bluesky get a nudge with the post ready to paste. Plug replies (one reply you write ahead) and reruns stay off until you switch them on.</p></div></li>
      <li><h3><button type="button" id="qb3" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="qa3">Is it just AI writing?<span class="pm" aria-hidden="true"><svg class="ico b"><use href="#i-plus"/></svg></span></button></h3>
        <div class="a" id="qa3" role="region" aria-labelledby="qb3" hidden><p>No. Sharpen is an editor: it cuts the fluff, tightens and reorders what’s left, and never adds a fact or a number. Rewrites in your voice run on Claude, only when you ask, and show every changed word. If a line needs a number you didn’t give, it writes [your number] and waits for yours.</p></div></li>
