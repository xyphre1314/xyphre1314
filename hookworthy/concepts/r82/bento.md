# Live Bento

**Idea:** one hero, then one bento of working product tiles with two-line captions, not a stack of sections.

**Scroll story**
- Hero: headline, CTAs, then the hero tile: the sharpen moment with 4 example pills, lit by the page's only beam.
- Row 1: Try (real engine, biggest) beside Previews (app switch, 2-post thread).
- Row 2: 11 PM to 8:40 AM on the night photo, a drafted reply you edit and send, a results note.
- Row 3: who it's for beside Studio (clients, comment, Approve, own-X and 8-voice lines).
- Row 4: the Never strip closes the grid.
- Pricing, live final input, footer.
- Phone: two columns, with Day and Results as a widget pair.

**Mobbin:** Cursor (wide tile over three), Resend (dark tiles that lead with UI), Retool (wide/narrow rhythm), Attio (icon cards, avoided).

**Weakest point:** Who and Results are static, and rows stretch to the tallest tile, so they show a gap under the caption.
