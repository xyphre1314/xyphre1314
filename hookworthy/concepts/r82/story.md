# Pinned Story

**Idea:** one Afterglow window stays pinned while chapters scroll beside it. Its sidebar is the chapter list, and the beam slides along its rim to whatever is new.

**Scroll story**
- Hero: the headline, with a draft whose fluff is squiggled.
- Sharpen: the fluff is struck through and the post lands (4 real examples).
- Previews: X, Threads, LinkedIn and Bluesky, plus the 2-post thread.
- Week: the 11:04 PM voice note arcs to an 8:40 AM slot.
- Replies, then Results, then Studio approval.
- Release: the "You…" people next to the real Try box. Then trust, pricing and the final.
- Phone: the window pinned on top, captions crossfading beneath it.
- Reduced motion or no JS: a static stack of chapter windows.

**Mobbin:** Linear Docs (active list plus one product pane), Ditto (stage rail beside the app), Retool (steps driving one screenshot).

**Weakest:** the desktop copy column is sparse between chapters.
