# Cinema

**Idea:** a film in scenes. Huge title cards sized to fill the width alternate with full-bleed product shots. Light is the cut.

**Scroll story**
1. "They read one line." The beam lands on the wide window (4 examples).
2. "Your facts stay." A live split screen: your draft and the sharp post at display size, with a seam of light between.
3. A swipeable strip of 4 app previews.
4. "Jot it at 11 PM." Night photo with the feed overlapping it.
5. Replies and results: sticky title, two stacked shots.
6. The cast: "You…" lines as credits.
7. Studio title card, then a window with a tab per client.
8. "We never post for you." 4 struck words.
9. Compact pricing.
10. The final card. Light lands on your live line.

**Mobbin:** Antimetal, Nike After Dark, Zaro/V7, Figma, Mistral.

**Weakest:** desktop runs 11.4 screens, and the Studio title card fits to only 29px on phones.
