  2. heycape · example account

    just wanted to share that i finally hit my first $10k month trading, took me 3 years lol

    my first $10k month trading took me 3 years.

    Cut the warm-up The number and the three years do the work. Lowercase stays, it’s how you write.

  3. Kenji · example founder

    Customer churn was reduced by 34% after the onboarding flow was redesigned by our team.

    Customer churn: down 34%. What changed: our team redesigned the onboarding flow.

    Led with the number 34% is the news. The how comes second.

  4. Nora · example newsletter writer

    honestly most design systems fail because nobody really owns them

    why do most design systems fail? nobody owns them.

    Asked, then answered The question opens a gap. Three words close it.
