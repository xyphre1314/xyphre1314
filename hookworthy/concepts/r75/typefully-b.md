# Variant B: Paper Trail

**Thesis:** Every rewrite leaves a paper trail (what was cut, what was kept, the move and why), so you trust the line that goes out.

**Hero:** "Good ideas die / in bad first lines." with an amber squiggle, beside a thermal receipt printing from the nav rule. It is a real Sharpen: your line, cut, kept, move and the new first line. "Print another" reprints it for each real example.

**Palette + type:** Warm paper, ink, a blue pencil plus amber marks, one black band. EB Garamond, Inter, Geist Mono receipts and Caveat margin notes, all local.

**Why depart:** A writer's desk, not a dashboard. Receipts and proof marks belong on paper, the opposite of Typefully's SaaS white.

**Not a clone:** Proof-sheet edits, a timetable in place of a feature grid, pricing set as receipts.

**Trade-offs:** Leaves the dark brand; Caveat must stay sparse.
