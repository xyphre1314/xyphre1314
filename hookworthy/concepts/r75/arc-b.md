# Show the Receipt (arc-b)

**Thesis:** Hookworthy as a sharp editor on warm paper. Soft words are quiet italic, the sharp line is bold ink, and every rewrite prints a receipt.

**Hero:** An editorial poster. "Good ideas die" is heavy condensed grotesk; "in bad first lines." is italic serif with the amber squiggle. A proof card strikes through the soft line and highlights the real rewrite in lime. A till receipt prints below it: move, why, cut, kept, word count. Visitors can switch between the four real rewrites, or try their own line.

**Palette + type:** Paper #F5EFE3, ink #17140F, lime #D4F13C as highlighter, ink pills, amber squiggles, pine for "better", pastel tiles. Bricolage Grotesque for sharp, Instrument Serif italic for soft, Geist Mono for receipts. The type does the before→after.

**Trade-offs:** It drops ultramarine, and lime only works behind ink. The fonts are inlined (no Google Fonts in the sandbox).
