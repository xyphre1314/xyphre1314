# Blue Pencil (Variant B, pushed)

**Thesis:** Hookworthy is the editor creators never had: a rough draft goes in, and a sharp first line comes out.

**Hero:** "Write it rough." in mono over a highlighter, then "Post it sharp." in big Garamond. On a taped galley proof, proofreader's marks draw themselves over a real draft (strikes, carets, cap marks, a move arrow). Then the set line and a pencilled receipt appear. It cycles through all four real examples.

**Palette + type:** Newsprint and ink. Ultramarine becomes the editor's blue pencil and amber the highlighter. EB Garamond, Geist Mono and Caveat.

**Why depart:** To "blue-pencil" means to edit. Paper shows the edit as craft and stands apart from dark SaaS tools, and the brand's two meaning colours survive.

**Not a clone:** It's an editor's desk, not a dashboard.

**Trade-offs:** It's light-first. Long rewrites (churn) get busy. Four faces need discipline.
