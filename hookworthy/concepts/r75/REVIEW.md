# Round 75 homepage concepts: results

Five designers (Apple, Linear, Awwwards, creator-tool, Arc/Airbnb) each built two concepts: A in the current brand, B pushing past it.
Two reviewers scored all ten without seeing each other's scores: an Awwwards juror (design 40, usability 30, creativity 20, content 10) and a conversion lead (clarity, hook, copy, proof, mobile, fit). Full scores are in the two JSON files next to this one.

Ranking = mean of both overall scores; ties broken by top-5 rank points (5 for 1st … 1 for 5th, summed across reviewers).
