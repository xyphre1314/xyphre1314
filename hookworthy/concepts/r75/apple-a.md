# Apple A: One Tap

**Thesis:** Show the Sharpen tap the way Apple shows a new button: one object, big, doing one thing perfectly.

**Hero:** "Same idea. / Sharper line." over a giant composer. A real soft line gets amber squiggles and the Sharpen pill presses itself. The old line shrinks into a struck caption, the sharp line rises in, and a receipt names the move and the facts kept. Four examples cycle, and you can pause them.

**Palette + type:** Current brand: dark, one ultramarine fill, amber for soft, green for better. Newsreader, Inter, Geist Mono. Every heading is a before→after: dim setup, bright punch.

**Not a clone:** The hero is the edit, not a composer screenshot. Receipts and the struck "Auto-DMs" list are Hookworthy's alone.

**Trade-offs:** Calm and centred, so it wins on precision more than spectacle. The page is long.
