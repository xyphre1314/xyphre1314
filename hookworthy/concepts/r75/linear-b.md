# Linear B: Hook Anatomy

**Thesis:** Draw a first line like an engineered part, with every edit labelled.

**Hero idea:** A poster with a type specimen on a precision stage of hairlines and crosshairs. The soft line has its cuts struck in amber. Below "One tap" is the sharp line at display size. Leader lines label each change: Cut · The warm-up, Move · Asked it as a question, Kept · Your facts. Avatars switch examples. Underneath: "Your idea is fine. / Your first line isn't."

**Palette + type (departure):** No blue. Near-black #060607, bone #F1EEE8 and a bone CTA pill. Colour only marks edits: amber cuts, green moves. Headlines are Inter Display 620. Newsreader only for the user's words; Geist Mono labels parts.

**Why:** The edit becomes the brand; serif always means your writing.

**Trade-off:** It drops ultramarine and the serif headlines. Leaders are desktop only; phones get chips.
