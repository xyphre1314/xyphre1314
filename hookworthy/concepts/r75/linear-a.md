# Linear A: Live Edit

**Thesis:** The product is the hero. It does its one job while you read the headline.

**Hero idea:** "Good ideas die / in bad first lines." is centred, with an amber squiggle. Below, the real composer is tilted and flattens as you scroll. heycape's soft line strikes through word by word, the gaps close and the sharp line lands with a green receipt ("Asked it as a question… 90 → 59"). The X preview updates live. Sharpen and ⌘↵ undo and replay it.

**Palette + type:** The current brand: #0B0C10, one ultramarine primary, amber for soft and green for sharper. Newsreader for display and the user's words, Inter for UI, Geist Mono for counts.

**Not a clone:** It crops to the first line and shows a word-level diff with a receipt, not a generic composer.

**Trade-off:** A familiar structure; it wins on craft, not surprise.
