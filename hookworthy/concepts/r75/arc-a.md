# Peel It Off (arc-a)

**Thesis:** Soft words are stickers on a good idea. Hookworthy peels them off and keeps your facts.

**Hero:** The punchline sits on a full-bleed ultramarine stage. Below it, heycape's real soft line has amber stickers on "so i think the" and "is honestly just". The @heycape_ cursor taps Sharpen, the stickers peel off into a draggable "Peeled off" tray, the line closes into the real rewrite, and a receipt slides out naming the move.

**Palette + type:** The current system, unchanged: #0B0C10, #2B52FF for the stage and primary, amber stickers, green inserts. Newsreader, Inter and Geist Mono. Warmth comes from tilt, springs, photos and faces.

**Not a clone:** The hero is an object you play with, not a screenshot in a browser frame.

**Trade-off:** The sections below the hero are calmer, closer to standard dark SaaS.
