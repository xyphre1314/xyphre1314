# The Cutting Floor (Variant A, brand system)

**Thesis:** Every soft word Hookworthy cuts hits the floor. What's still standing is your hook.

**Hero:** "Good ideas die / in bad first lines." set huge. A real heycape draft sharpens in one tap. Cut words fall and pile up on a cutting floor you can poke. Hovering a word shows why it was cut.

**Palette + type:** Brand dark, with ultramarine for the one primary and the full stop. Amber squiggles mark soft words, green marks kept ones. Newsreader with italic second lines, Inter, Geist Mono for counts and times. One paper receipt is the accent.

**Not a clone:** The edit is the art direction. Every feature title is sharpened too, with the generic SaaS line struck through beneath it.

**Trade-offs:** The floor is decorative and pointer-driven. The features grid is more conventional than the hero.
