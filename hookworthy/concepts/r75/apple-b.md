# Apple B: Blue Field

**Thesis:** Soft lines blur into the feed. The page makes you feel the one worth stopping for before you read a word.

**Hero:** A full-bleed ultramarine field. Faint soft-opening posts drift upward, and one crisp white post sits still in the middle, sharpened, its old line struck above. "Write the line / they stop for." is set huge.

**Palette + type (a deliberate departure):** The blue becomes the stage instead of a button, deepened to #2448F2 so white text passes AA. Paper and near-black sections set the rhythm. Inter at its display optical size replaces Newsreader, so headlines read like posts. Amber and green keep their meaning.

**Not a clone:** Typefully sells a calm editor. This sells the outcome in the feed.

**Trade-offs:** It leaves the serif voice behind. Blue is loud, so it appears three times only.
