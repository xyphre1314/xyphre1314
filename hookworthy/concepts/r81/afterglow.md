# Afterglow: brand system

**Tokens:** ground #08090A · soft white #F7F8F8 · dim #A4A8AF (heading line 2, sub) · meta #8D929A · hairline rgba(255,255,255,.07) · ember #FFAA80, used only as light and the fluff underline, never a fill.

**Type:** Inter only, from one opsz file. Display (opsz 32) for headings: H1 80/1.0 (phone 10.4vw), H2 56/1.05, 600, −0.04em, two lines with line 2 dimmer, no gradient text. Text (opsz 14–17) for UI: 17 lede, 15 body, 13 meta.

**Surfaces:** glass panels with a 1px gradient hairline, a 1px inner top highlight and three stacked shadows. Fine grain over everything.

**Light:** at most one beam per section (only the hero and the final have one). It enters off-canvas, misses text and lands on product, where the rim turns into a warm specular line. Text over light is measured to AA.

**Buttons:** solid white pill (primary), frosted glass pill (secondary), hairline segmented pills.

**Post cards:** X-dark #000 with a #2F3336 hairline, #E7E9EA text and an "Example" tag.
